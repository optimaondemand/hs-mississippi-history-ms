📖 BACKGROUND

In 1927, the Illinois Central Railroad Company photographed the flooding along its lines from Memphis, Tennessee, south to Delhi, Louisiana, documenting the scale of the disaster as it happened. The Mississippi Department of Archives and History preserves 124 of these photographs today.

As you view the photographs, pay attention to what they show being moved, rescued, or left behind, and to who appears in each image.

💡 VIEW THE PHOTOGRAPHS
View the full digitized collection at the Mississippi Department of Archives and History Digital Archives. Browse at least five photographs before completing the paired exercise.
These are real photographs taken during the flood itself, not staged reenactments or later illustrations.
