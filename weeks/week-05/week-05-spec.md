   c) Native nations had no goods of their own to trade

2. **The deerskin trade created economic dependence:**
   a) only for European colonies, never for Native nations
   b) in both directions, as Native nations relied more on European goods and colonies relied on deerskin income *(correct)*
   c) only for Native nations, never for European colonies
   d) for neither side, since deerskins had no real value in Europe

3. **French support for the Choctaw during Choctaw-Chickasaw conflict is best explained as:**

## Primary Source Exercise 5.1 — The Treaty of Hopewell (1786)

4 questions. Types (never shown to students): conceptual, historical causation, counterfactual, conceptual.

1. **(Conceptual)** Why would the newly independent United States want to establish, in writing, that it alone (not individual states or private citizens) held authority over trade and treaties with the Choctaw Nation?
2. **(Historical causation)** Why would the United States seek a formal peace treaty with the Choctaw so soon after the Revolution ended, rather than assuming peaceful relations by default?
3. **(Counterfactual)** Suppose the Choctaw Nation had refused to sign any treaty with the United States at all. How might relations between the two have developed differently in the following decades?
4. **(Conceptual)** A treaty is an agreement between two parties who both consent to its terms. What does the very existence of this treaty suggest about how the United States, at least officially, needed to treat the Choctaw Nation in 1786?

## Primary Source Exercise 5.2 — The Mississippi Band of Choctaw Indians' Constitution (1945)

4 questions. Types (never shown to students): conceptual, historical causation, counterfactual, conceptual.

1. **(Conceptual)** What does the fact that this document was written by the Choctaw people themselves, rather than negotiated with an outside government like the Treaty of Hopewell, suggest about the difference between the two documents?
2. **(Historical causation)** Why might it have taken until 1945, roughly 115 years after removal began, for the Mississippi Band of Choctaw Indians to formally organize a written constitution?
3. **(Counterfactual)** Suppose Choctaw people had not remained in Mississippi after removal in the 1830s. How might the existence of this 1945 constitution, and everything it represents, be different?
4. **(Conceptual)** Does the existence of a modern constitutional government change how you think about the earlier chapters of Choctaw history you're studying this week? Explain your reasoning.

**What you'll need:** VR headset (desktop/mobile fallback available), this week's two lessons and two primary sources already read.
