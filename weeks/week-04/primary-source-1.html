📖 BACKGROUND

Antoine-Simon Le Page du Pratz was a French planter and colonial administrator who lived in Louisiana for sixteen years in the early 1700s, including years spent living near the Natchez, where he learned their language and observed their society directly. He later published a detailed history of the colony that remains one of the most valuable firsthand accounts of Natchez life that survives today.

Before reading any historian's or observer's claims, it is worth asking what actually qualifies them to make those claims. This excerpt shows how du Pratz himself, and the account's own opening pages, established his authority to write about what he saw.

📜 FROM THE BOOK
"The History of Louisiana, which we here present to the public, was wrote by a planter of sixteen years experience in that country, who had likewise the advantage of being overseer or director of the public plantations, both when they belonged to the company, and afterwards when they fell to the crown."
"It was with this embarkation I passed over to Louisiana."

Excerpt transcribed from the edition hosted by Project Gutenberg.
