## Review Activity — "Mapping the Quarter"

**Format:** Comparative timeline and chart, submitted as a document (per `optima-m365-skills` conventions for the eventual Canvas build).

**Instructions to students:** Build a single timeline spanning Weeks 1–8, plotting at least eight events in chronological order (for example: Columbus's 1492 voyage, La Salle's 1682 claim, De Soto's 1540–41 expedition, Iberville's 1699 settlement, the Code Noir's 1724 issuance, the 1763 Treaty of Paris, the 1779 Spanish seizure of West Florida, and the Natchez's 1729 loss of independence). For each event, add one sentence explaining why it mattered, not just what happened.

Then complete a comparison chart with three columns, Spain, France, and England, and four rows: Primary Motivation, Relationship with Native Nations, What the Power Left Behind, and Length of Control in Mississippi. Fill in each cell using specific details from the quarter's lessons and primary sources.

**Success criteria:** The timeline is in correct chronological order with accurate dates. The comparison chart uses specific, accurate details rather than vague generalizations. Explanations connect events to causes or consequences discussed in the lessons, not just restate what happened.
